# qa_028 — How to handle contradictory sources?

TYPE: PROCEDURAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: kg:contradiction
TTL: 180

QUESTION: How to handle contradictory sources?
ANSWER: Keep both, record the conflict, rank by recency and authority, surface uncertainty.
EXAMPLE: Two papers disagree; show both and the newer one wins by date.
