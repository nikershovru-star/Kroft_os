# qa_013 — What is modularity?

TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: clean-arch
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is modularity?
ANSWER: Decomposing a system so parts have low coupling and high cohesion.
EXAMPLE: Kernel, services, and adapters are separate layers with clear boundaries.
