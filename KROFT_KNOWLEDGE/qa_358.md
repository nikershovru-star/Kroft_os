# qa_358 — What is an ad hominem?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: phil:logic
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is an ad hominem?
ANSWER: Attacking the person instead of the argument; a fallacy.
EXAMPLE: Dismiss the claim by insulting.
