# qa_122 — What is expected value?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high

QUESTION: What is expected value?
ANSWER: The probability weighted sum of outcomes; the rational long run average of a choice.
EXAMPLE: Fifty percent win of ten minus cost gives the value.
