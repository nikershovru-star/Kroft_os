TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:logic

QUESTION: What is a logical fallacy?
ANSWER: A flaw in reasoning where the conclusion does not follow from the premises.
EXAMPLE: Appeal to authority is not proof of a claim.
