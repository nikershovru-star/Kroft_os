# qa_137 — What is anchoring?

DOMAIN: psychology
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: psy: bias
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is anchoring?
ANSWER: Over relying on the first number seen when estimating; it shifts later judgments.
EXAMPLE: A high list price makes a discount seem large.
