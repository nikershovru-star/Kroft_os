# qa_016 — What is causation versus correlation?

TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: reasoning:cause
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is causation versus correlation?
ANSWER: Correlation is co-occurrence; causation is that one event produces another.
EXAMPLE: Sales rose after ads; that does not prove ads caused the rise.
