# qa_068 — When to persist versus keep in-memory?

TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: self:KROFT
TTL: 365

QUESTION: When to persist versus keep in-memory?
ANSWER: Persist across restarts what must survive; keep ephemeral state in memory.
EXAMPLE: Graph and index persist; transient caches stay in memory.
