# qa_092 — Why is falsifiability important?

DOMAIN: scientific-method
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:science
TTL: 365

QUESTION: Why is falsifiability important?
ANSWER: A claim that cannot be tested cannot be corrected by evidence; falsifiability keeps science self-correcting.
EXAMPLE: Astrology avoids testable claims; it is not falsifiable.
