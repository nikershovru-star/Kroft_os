# qa_262 — What is mitigation versus resolution?

DOMAIN: incident-response
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: sre:incident

QUESTION: What is mitigation versus resolution?
ANSWER: Mitigation reduces impact now; resolution removes the cause later.
EXAMPLE: Reroute traffic to mitigate; fix the bug to resolve.
