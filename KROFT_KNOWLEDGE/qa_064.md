TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: self:KROFT

QUESTION: When to cache versus recompute embeddings?
ANSWER: Cache when the same summary repeats; recompute only for new summaries.
EXAMPLE: Five identical episodes embed once, then hit the cache.
