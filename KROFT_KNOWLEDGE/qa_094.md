# qa_094 — What is replication?

DOMAIN: scientific-method
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:science
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is replication?
ANSWER: Repeating a study to see if the result holds; failed replication weakens a claim.
EXAMPLE: A finding that disappears on repeat is suspect.
