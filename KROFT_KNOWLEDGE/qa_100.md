# qa_100 — What is a theory versus a law?

DOMAIN: scientific-method
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:science
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is a theory versus a law?
ANSWER: A law states what happens; a theory explains why; both are evidence-backed, not opinions.
EXAMPLE: Gravity is a law; evolution is a theory with mechanism.
