# qa_126 — What is a sunk cost?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:decision
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is a sunk cost?
ANSWER: A cost already paid that should not affect future choices; only marginal future matters.
EXAMPLE: Do not finish a bad movie because you paid for it.
