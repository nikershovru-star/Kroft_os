# qa_146 — What is coreference?

DOMAIN: linguistics
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: ling: discourse
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is coreference?
ANSWER: When expressions in text refer to the same entity; needed for understanding.
EXAMPLE: He and the man point to one person.
