# qa_359 — How to spot a loaded question?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: PROCEDURAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:logic
TTL: 180

QUESTION: How to spot a loaded question?
ANSWER: A question that presumes an unproven claim; reject the premise first.
EXAMPLE: Have you stopped cheating presumes guilt.
