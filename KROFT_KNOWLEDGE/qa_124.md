# qa_124 — What is satisficing?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:decision
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is satisficing?
ANSWER: Choosing the first option good enough rather than the optimal one; saves search cost.
EXAMPLE: Accept a house above threshold instead of the perfect one.
