# qa_003 — What is a thread?

TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: textbook:OSTEP
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is a thread?
ANSWER: A unit of execution within a process; threads of one process share its memory.
EXAMPLE: Two threads increment a shared counter and must synchronize.
