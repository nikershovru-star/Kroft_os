# qa_370 — What is inattentional blindness?

DOMAIN: psychology
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: psy:attention

QUESTION: What is inattentional blindness?
ANSWER: Missing visible events while attention is on another task.
EXAMPLE: Count passes, miss the gorilla.
