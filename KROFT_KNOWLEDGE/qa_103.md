DOMAIN: causal
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: paper:DoCalculus
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is the do-operator?
ANSWER: Intervention notation do(X) means forcing X, not merely observing it; separates cause from association.
EXAMPLE: do(smoke) sets smoking; observing smokers selects them.
