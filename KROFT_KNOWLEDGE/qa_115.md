# qa_115 — What is a false dilemma?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:logic

QUESTION: What is a false dilemma?
ANSWER: Presenting only two options when more exist; reduces a spectrum to forced choice.
EXAMPLE: You are with us or against us ignores neutrality.
