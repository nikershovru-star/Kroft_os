# qa_133 — What is the availability heuristic?

DOMAIN: psychology
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: psy: bias
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is the availability heuristic?
ANSWER: Judging likelihood by how easily examples come to mind; vivid events seem more common.
EXAMPLE: Plane crashes feel likelier than car crashes.
