# qa_230 — When to auto-correct versus ask?

TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: meta:correction

QUESTION: When to auto-correct versus ask?
ANSWER: Auto-correct cheap reversible errors; ask a human on irreversible or safety critical ones.
EXAMPLE: Fix a typo alone; confirm a deletion.
