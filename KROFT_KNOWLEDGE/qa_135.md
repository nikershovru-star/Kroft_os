# qa_135 — What is intrinsic motivation?

DOMAIN: psychology
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: psy: motivation

QUESTION: What is intrinsic motivation?
ANSWER: Doing something for its own sake rather than for a reward; often more durable.
EXAMPLE: A hobby is done for interest, not pay.
