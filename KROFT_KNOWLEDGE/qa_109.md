# qa_109 — Why is correlation not enough for policy?

DOMAIN: causal
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: paper:Causal
TTL: 365

QUESTION: Why is correlation not enough for policy?
ANSWER: Acting on a correlation may fail if a confounder drives both; policy needs the true cause.
EXAMPLE: Building hospitals because sick people cluster there worsens outcomes elsewhere.
