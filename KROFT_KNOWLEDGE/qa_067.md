# qa_067 — When to split versus merge a module?

TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: clean-arch
TTL: 365

QUESTION: When to split versus merge a module?
ANSWER: Split when responsibilities diverge and coupling rises; merge when a module is too small to stand alone.
EXAMPLE: A tiny helper merges into its caller; a growing service splits by capability.
