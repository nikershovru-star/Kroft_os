DOMAIN: psychology
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high

QUESTION: What is cognitive load?
ANSWER: The mental effort used in working memory; overload impairs learning and decisions.
EXAMPLE: Too many steps at once exceed working memory.
