# qa_120 — What is a strawman?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: phil:logic
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is a strawman?
ANSWER: Misrepresenting a position as weaker than it is, then refuting the weak version.
EXAMPLE: Caricature the claim, then defeat the caricature.
