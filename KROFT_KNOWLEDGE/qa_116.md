# qa_116 — What is confirmation bias?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: FACTUAL
CONFIDENCE: medium
PROVENANCE: phil:logic
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is confirmation bias?
ANSWER: The tendency to notice and keep evidence that fits a belief and ignore contrary evidence.
EXAMPLE: A believer keeps headlines that confirm and skips refutations.
