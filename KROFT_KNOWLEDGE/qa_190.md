# qa_190 — When in-memory versus persisted memory?

DOMAIN: memory-systems
TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: kg:memory

QUESTION: When in-memory versus persisted memory?
ANSWER: Keep ephemeral state in memory; persist what must survive restart or be shared.
EXAMPLE: Cache stays live; graph and skills persist.
