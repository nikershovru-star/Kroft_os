# qa_113 — What is the burden of proof?

DOMAIN: critical-thinking
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:logic
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is the burden of proof?
ANSWER: The claimant must supply evidence; absence of disproof is not proof.
EXAMPLE: Extraordinary claims need extraordinary evidence.
