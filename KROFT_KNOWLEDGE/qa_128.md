# qa_128 — What is opportunity cost?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: CONCEPTUAL
CONFIDENCE: high
PROVENANCE: phil:decision
TTL: 365

QUESTION: What is opportunity cost?
ANSWER: The value of the next best alternative given up by a choice.
EXAMPLE: An hour of leisure costs an hour of work.
