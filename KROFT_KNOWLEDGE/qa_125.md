# qa_125 — When to satisfice versus optimize?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high

QUESTION: When to satisfice versus optimize?
ANSWER: Satisfice under high search cost or uncertain value; optimize when the gain is large and measurable.
EXAMPLE: Pick lunch fast; optimize a rare investment.
