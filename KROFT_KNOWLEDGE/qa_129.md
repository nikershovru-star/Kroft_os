# qa_129 — When to decide under uncertainty?

DOMAIN: decision
TYPE: DECISIONAL
CONFIDENCE: high

QUESTION: When to decide under uncertainty?
ANSWER: Use max-min for safety critical cases and expected value when the distribution is known.
EXAMPLE: Surgery uses worst case; betting uses averages.
